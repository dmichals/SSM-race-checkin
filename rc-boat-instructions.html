Race Check-In & Finish Tool

Committee boat quick-reference — Sailing Series Manager (SSM)

1. Before You Leave the Dock (need signal)

  • Open the check-in tool: dmichals.github.io/SSM-race-checkin/race-checkin. Leave it open — do not close the tab once you're out on the water.
  • Tap Refresh roster once. This loads all boat names and sail numbers from SSM and saves them to your phone, so autocomplete keeps working with no signal.

2. Race Setup & During the Race (works with zero signal)

  • Enter the Committee Boat name and your name under Recorded By at the top of the Race Setup box.
  • In the Race Setup box, set today's Starting Area, Weather Mark, and Courses for JAM and Spinnaker fleets by tapping the correct button for each.
  • Check the three Section Start Times — they default to 18:45:00 (Section 4), 18:50:00 (Section 3), 18:55:00 (Section 2). Edit any of them if today's schedule is different.
  • Check a boat in: type the sail number in the Sail # box. The boat name should appear automatically underneath — confirm it's the right boat, then tap Check In.
  • Record a finish: find the boat's card in the list and tap the orange Finish button the instant it crosses. This stamps the current time.
  • Fix a finish time: if a time needs adjusting, tap Edit time under that boat's finish time, change it, and tap Save.
  • Mark DNS / DNF / DSQ: tap the matching button on the boat's card. Tap the same button again to clear it if it was marked by mistake.

All of this is saved directly on your phone. Nothing needs signal until you sync.

3. Back in Cell Range

  • Tap Sync to SSM once. This sends every check-in and finish time into the spreadsheet.
  • The message under the buttons confirms how many records were synced. If it fails, just try again — nothing is lost, and already-synced boats won't be sent twice.
  • Once you've confirmed the data landed in SSM, tap Clear data — start new race before the next race. It will warn you first if anything is still unsynced.

4. Safety Net

  • Tap Export CSV backup any time to save a copy of everything on the phone itself — do this before clearing data if you're at all unsure it synced.
  • Keep a stopwatch or notepad on board as a paper backup in case a phone dies or gets wet.
  • The status bar at the top (red/green dot) shows whether the phone currently has signal — it's just informational, you can keep working either way.
